Frequently asked questions

Is WishLink really free?

Yes. Creating and sharing birthday pages is completely free, with no sign-up and no app to install. Free pages carry a small “Made with WishLink” line at the bottom.

How does the person open my surprise?

You send them one link (WhatsApp works best). The page asks for the secret PIN you chose — tell them the PIN in your message. Once it opens, the celebration starts: music, confetti, cake, your photos, a gift box and your letter.

Can I add our photos?

Yes — up to 4 photos. They are resized in your browser and stored securely with your page on our servers, then shown as an auto-sliding gallery the birthday person can also swipe through.

Do I need to install an app or create an account?

No. WishLink works entirely in the browser, on any phone or computer. There are no accounts and no passwords to remember — just the PIN on each page.

What occasions does WishLink support?

Birthday pages are live now with 15 designs. Anniversary, wedding, baby, Eid and New Year pages are in the works — the same idea, made for each celebration.
