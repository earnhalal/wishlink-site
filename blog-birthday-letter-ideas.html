Birthday Letter Ideas: What to Write in a Birthday Letter

The wish is the headline — the letter is the part they re-read at 2am. Here is a simple structure that works for anyone, plus three ready examples.

The 4-part structure that never fails

  1. Open with today. “Happy Birthday! Today is all about you, so let me say the things I never say out loud…”
  2. One specific memory. Not “you are a great friend” — the night, the trip, the joke. Specific is what makes people cry (the good kind).
  3. What they mean to you. One or two honest sentences. Keep it plain; plain is powerful.
  4. A wish for their year. Health, courage, the thing they are working toward. End with your name — a letter needs a signature.

Example 1 — Sweet & simple

Dear Sam,

Happy Birthday! I will keep this short and sweet: thank you for being exactly who you are. Your laugh has rescued more of my bad days than you will ever know.

May this year bring you good health, true happiness, and everything you wished for when the candles went out.

— Alex

Example 2 — For a best friend

Happy Birthday, partner in crime.

Another year of us: the plans that failed, the food that did not, and the 3am conversations that fixed everything. I do not say it enough — life is measurably better with you in it.

This year, I hope you get everything you have been quietly working for. I will be in the front row, embarrassing you with applause.

— Your favourite person (obviously)

Example 3 — Short & loving

Dear Ammi,

Happy Birthday. Everything good in me started with you. I pray this year returns to you a little of the comfort you have given everyone else your whole life.

With all my love.

Three mistakes to avoid

  • Copying a poem that does not sound like you. They can tell. Simple and true wins.
  • Only jokes. One laugh is great; a letter that is all jokes says nothing. Add one honest line.
  • Writing a novel. 120–200 words is the sweet spot on a phone screen.
