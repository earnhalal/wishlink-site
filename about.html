BIRTHDAY SURPRISE PAGES

About WishLink

WishLink makes one thing, and makes it with love: little web pages that turn a birthday into a moment.

A birthday message on chat is easy to send — and just as easy to forget. A WishLink page is different. It arrives as a single link, locked with a secret PIN only the two of you know. Behind the PIN: music, confetti, a cake with candles to blow out, photos of your favourite moments sliding by, a hidden gift box, and a letter written just for them.

How it works

  1. You fill a tiny form — their name, your name, a wish and a letter. Ready-made wishes and letters are built in if you are stuck for words.
  2. You pick a design — 15 themes, from Midnight Gold to Candy Pop, each with its own PIN screen, loading animation and colours.
  3. You add photos (optional) — up to 4, shown as an auto-sliding gallery.
  4. You share one link — on WhatsApp, Messenger, anywhere. They type the PIN, and the celebration begins.

What it costs

Nothing. WishLink is free — no sign-up, no app to install, no watermarks on the moment itself (free pages carry one small “Made with WishLink” line). We are a small independent studio, and WishLink is supported by advertising and by the optional extras we are building for people who want even more.

What is coming

Birthday pages are just the beginning. The same little celebration is being made for anniversaries, weddings, new babies, Eid and New Year — every occasion where one person deserves to feel celebrated by another.

Who makes WishLink?

WishLink is built and run by a small independent team that loves friendly tools for the web. Questions, ideas or a page you need removed? Talk to us — we read everything.
