How to Make a Birthday Surprise Page Online (Free, in 2 Minutes)

A chat message takes ten seconds — and is forgotten by lunch. A surprise page takes two minutes and gets remembered all year. Here is exactly how to make one.

What you need

Just a phone and a browser. No app, no account, no payment. You will write four small things: their name, your name, a birthday wish and a short letter. If words do not come easily, WishLink has 85+ ready wishes and letters built in — pick one and tweak a line or two so it sounds like you.

Step 1 — Pick a design first

Design sets the mood before a single word is read. WishLink has 15 designs, and each one changes everything: the colours, the PIN screen, even the loading animation. Elegant and grown-up? Rose Royal or Emerald Royal. Fun and loud? Candy Pop. Soft and dreamy? Pastel Sky or Lavender Dream. Choose for them, not for you.

Step 2 — Set a secret PIN

The PIN is the little lock on the gift box. Use something only the two of you would guess — a shared joke, the year you met, their nickname in numbers. You will send the PIN with the link (“PIN: 1432 😉”), and that tiny moment of unlocking is what makes it feel like a present instead of a post.

Step 3 — Write the wish (or steal one of ours)

The wish is the headline of the page. Short and true beats long and flowery. “Another year of you — and the world is better for it” will land harder than three paragraphs of poetry that does not sound like you. Need inspiration? We collected 25 wishes for a best friend you can copy.

Step 4 — Add photos

Up to 4 photos, shown as an auto-sliding gallery on the page. Pick moments, not portraits: the laughing one, the trip one, the terrible-haircut one. Photos are resized in your browser and saved inside the page. More tips in our photo guide.

Step 5 — Hide something in the gift box

Every page has a gift box that reveals one hidden line when tapped. Make it count: a promise, an inside joke, or the announcement of the real gift (“check your door at 6pm 👀”).

Step 6 — Send one link

Tap create and you get a short link with their name in it. Send it on WhatsApp a minute before midnight, first thing in the morning, or whenever the moment is right. Prefer a file? You can also download the page and send it as a document.

That is the whole recipe: design, PIN, wish, letter, photos, gift, link. Two minutes of effort, a whole day of them smiling at their phone.
