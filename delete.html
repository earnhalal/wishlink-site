Delete a page

Created a page and want it gone? You can delete it yourself — no need to contact us.

You need two things: the page link and the edit token you were given when the page was created (it is also kept on the device that created the page). Deleting removes the page and its photos permanently.

Lost your token? Contact us with the page link and we will help. Pages made before edit tokens existed can also be removed by contacting us.
