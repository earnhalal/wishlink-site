How to Add Photos to a Birthday Page (and Choose Ones That Land)

Words tell them you remembered. Photos prove it. Here is how photos work on a WishLink page, and how to pick four that do the heavy lifting.

How it works

In the maker, tap “Add photos” and choose up to 4 pictures. They are resized right in your browser — no uploads to social media, nothing public — and saved inside your page. When the birthday person opens it, the photos appear as a gallery that slides on its own every few seconds, with a gentle zoom as each one shows. They can also swipe through at their own pace, or tap the arrows.

The 4-photo formula

  1. The classic — the two of you (or them) smiling, the “us” photo. Opens the show.
  2. The laugh — mid-laugh, unposed, slightly imperfect. This is the one they will screenshot.
  3. The throwback — the oldest one you have. Nostalgia is rocket fuel.
  4. The recent one — from this year, to land the story in the present: “and here we still are.”

Practical tips

  • Faces over places. A famous landmark is nice; their face in front of it is the photo.
  • Bright beats dark. Phone screens forgive a lot, but a bright photo always wins.
  • One person per story. Group photos are great, but make sure they are findable in the frame.
  • Mind the crop. The gallery shows photos in a wide frame — landscape photos fill it best; portraits still work, centred on the face.
  • Permission. Only add photos you have the right to share, of people who would be happy to see them there.

No photos? No problem

A page without photos still carries the full celebration — design, music, cake, gift and letter. Photos are the amplifier, not the message. But if you have even one good picture of them, add it: it is the difference between a lovely page and their page.
