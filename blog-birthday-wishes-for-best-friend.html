BIRTHDAY SURPRISE PAGES

Wishes & Words · 15 November 2026

25 Heartfelt Birthday Wishes for Your Best Friend

For the person who knows all your stories — and is in half of them. Copy any of these straight into your surprise page, or mix two together.

Sweet & heartfelt

1. Another year of you — and the world is better for it. Happy Birthday!
2. To the one who makes ordinary days feel like a celebration — happiest of birthdays.
3. Some people make the world softer just by being in it. You are one of them.
4. May this year give you back a fraction of the kindness you give everyone else.
5. Happy Birthday to my favourite chapter in every story.

Short & sweet

6. Older? Yes. Wiser? Debatable. Loved? Completely.
7. You were born, and the group chat has never been the same. 🎉
8. Cake today, dreams all year. Happy Birthday!
9. Same time next year? I will bring the candles, you bring the wishes.
10. HBD to the human version of a Friday evening.

Funny

11. Congratulations on being one year closer to becoming the eccentric old person we always knew you would be.
12. I got you the best gift: my presence. You are welcome. 🎁
13. Age is just a number — in your case, a surprisingly large one.
14. Happy Birthday! Do not worry about getting older. I will always be younger than you.
15. You do not look a day over fabulous.

For a long-distance bestie

16. Miles apart, same team. Happy Birthday — I am celebrating you from here.
17. If I could, I would deliver this wish in person with cake. Until then, this page is me knocking on your screen.
18. Distance test: failed to keep us apart for another whole year. Happy Birthday!
19. Somewhere between your timezone and mine, it is always your birthday. Enjoy both.
20. Sending you a hug in link form. Open carefully — it is a big one.

Make-it-count wishes

21. May every dream you are quietly working on find its way to you this year.
22. This is your year. I have seen how hard you work when nobody is watching.
23. New year, new orbit. Fly higher than every wish you whispered last year.
24. Whatever this year brings, you will not face any of it alone. That is a promise, not a wish.
25. Happy Birthday. Thank you for being exactly who you are — please do not change a thing.

One last tip

The best wish is the one that could only be for them. Take any wish above and add one specific line — a memory, a nickname, a plan. Thirty seconds of editing turns a good wish into their wish. Then hide it behind a PIN in a surprise page and watch it land.
