BIRTHDAY SURPRISE PAGES

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This policy explains what information WishLink (wishlink.pp.ua) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. WishLink is operated by the WishLink team (“we”, “us”). By using the site you agree to this policy.

1. Information you create

When you create a surprise page, the content you enter — names, wish, letter, gift line, design choice and any photos you add — is stored securely on our servers (Google Firebase) so that your share link can open the page for the person you send it to. Photos are resized in your browser and stored in secure cloud storage at private, hard-to-guess addresses — they are not inside the page file. Your PIN is never stored as plain text: it is stored only as a salted cryptographic hash, and our server checks the PIN before any page content is released. We do not require an account, and we never ask for passwords, payment details or government IDs.

2. Information collected automatically

Our hosting provider (Vercel) and database provider (Google Firebase) may process standard technical information such as IP address, browser type and timestamps in server logs, which is necessary to operate and secure the service. WishLink pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of serving those fonts.

3. Cookies and advertising

WishLink itself does not set tracking cookies. Advertising never appears on birthday surprise pages. On our homepage and blog pages only, we may display advertising provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, and learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com. If you are in the EEA/UK, ads may be served in non-personalised form where consent is required and not given.

4. How content is used

Page content is used only to display the page to people who have the link and the PIN. We do not sell personal information. We do not read your letters for marketing. Content may be removed if it violates our Terms of Use.

5. Sharing a link is your choice

Anyone who has both the link and the PIN can open a page. Please share links and PINs only with the people you intend, and only add photos you have the right to share.

6. Data retention and removal

Pages and their photos are kept until the creator deletes them. If you created a page, you can delete it yourself at any time on the Delete a page page using the private edit token you received when the page was created — the page and its photos are removed permanently. Every surprise page also has a “Report this page” button; pages reported repeatedly are reviewed and removed if they break our terms. You can also email us from the contact page. If a page about you was created by someone else and you want it removed, contact us the same way.

7. Children

WishLink is a general-audience tool. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Birthday pages are created by users; if you believe a page contains a child's personal information that should be removed, contact us.

8. Security

We use reputable providers and encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, so please do not put sensitive personal information (passwords, financial details, ID numbers) inside a page.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the service grows. The “last updated” date above will change, and significant changes will be noted on this page.
